# Caption Reading Speed Study: 387 Videos Measured

Reading-speed measurements for the captions of 387 videos: 139 NASA videos and 248 MIT OpenCourseWare lectures, each caption file parsed and measured against Netflix, BBC and DCMP caption guidelines.

Published by [Mark Studios](https://www.markstudios.com) with the full write-up, method and limitations at **https://www.markstudios.com/research/caption-reading-speed-study**.

## Files

One row per video: source, group, title or course, date, number of captions, minutes, words per minute overall, median characters per second, the percentage of captions over 20 characters a second, over 160 words a minute, with a line over 42 characters, shown under 5/6 of a second, shown over 7 seconds, with a gap under 2 frames, the longest line, and the caption file URL.

Files in `data/` are copied from the live page on 2026-10-25; the page is the source of truth and is re-verified quarterly.
